Welcome

A grand house
on a quiet lake.

Grand Hotel Begnas stands on Begnas Sadak, close enough to the water that mornings begin with mist on the lake and the Annapurnas turning gold. Inside: thirteen unhurried rooms, a restaurant and bar, and a staff guests keep writing home about.

It is the Pokhara of twenty years ago — no crowds, no noise — twenty minutes from the city.
