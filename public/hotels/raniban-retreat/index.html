Welcome to Raniban Retreat

A quieter Pokhara,
in the Queen’s Forest

Raniban — the Queen’s Forest — shelters a small boutique retreat on the hill of the World Peace Pagoda. Fourteen rooms and Gurung-style cottages face one unbroken frame: the Annapurna range, Phewa Lake and the whole Pokhara valley. The retreat runs on sunshine, pours spring water, and is cared for by the neighbourhood it sits in.

Good to know

Before you climb up

By road in about 30–35 minutes from Pokhara International Airport, or a taxi from Lakeside (around NPR 1,500). Walkers can climb the forest trail — roughly 522 stone steps from the lakeside up through Raniban forest, with the Peace Pagoda steps continuing from our gate. We can arrange pickup on request.

Check-in from 2:00 PM, check-out until 12:00 PM. Early arrivals are welcome to relax on the terrace while the room is prepared.
